# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact directly.
- Respect privacy, especially around food, body, and health information.
- Do not shame people for dietary choices, health conditions, or tracking goals.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this policy. Report concerns privately through the repository's security reporting channel.

This compact policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
