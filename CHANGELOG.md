## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-18

### Added

- Review-first meal photo analysis with structured OpenAI vision output.
- Separate identity, portion, and nutrition confidence fields.
- Editable macro review before persistence.
- Local SQLite history that does not retain raw photos.
- Optional authenticated Supabase sync with per-user RLS.
- Product decisions, data dictionary, threat model, contributor guidance, and CI.
